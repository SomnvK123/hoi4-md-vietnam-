# Air Defence - Air Force roster steps (plan: VIE_air_force_implementation_plan.md). Step 0 (Soat and the first four
# high_command entries, plus retiring the two mis-assigned upstream air chiefs) lives in VIE_md_on_actions_startup.txt.
# Called every month from VIE_md_on_actions.txt, so each date is reached within a month. Only one-way operations:
# a character is recruited once and retired once, never brought back. Blocks stay in date order on purpose: a late

VIE_event_scheduler_air = {
